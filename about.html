My Story

I have always been someone who enjoys understanding how things work. From a young age I was constantly taking things apart to see what was inside and trying to figure out how everything worked.

While this sometimes tested my parents' patience, I usually managed to put things back together and learn something from the process. That curiosity naturally developed into an interest in computers, technology, and problem solving.

Technology & Learning

Technology has always been something I enjoy exploring. I like learning new skills, experimenting with different systems, and understanding how things work rather than simply using them.

This has led me into programming, hardware, servers, networking, cybersecurity, and self-hosted systems.

My Homelab

My homelab comes from the same mindset I have always had: being self-sufficient and having control over the technology I use.

I enjoy building systems exactly how I want them, learning how services connect together, and understanding where my data is stored.

Motorcycles & Mechanics

Motorcycles have been a major interest throughout my life. Growing up, my dad was a car mechanic and introduced me to the engineering side of vehicles.

Attending events such as the NW200 and other road races around Ireland helped build my interest in motorcycles, performance, and engineering.

I became a qualified motorcycle mechanic because I wanted to understand how bikes worked, maintain them myself, and improve them.

Camping & Exploring

Outside of technology, I enjoy spending time outdoors, travelling, and exploring new places.

Camping gives me a chance to disconnect, experience new places, and challenge myself to be more self-sufficient.

It is also a great balance alongside spending time with computers and technology.

Photography

Photography is another hobby I enjoy because it allows me to capture places, experiences, and moments that I would otherwise forget.

Whether travelling, exploring outdoors, attending events, or finding interesting things around me, I enjoy improving my skills and learning how to tell a story through images.

Gaming & Creativity

Gaming has been a big part of my life and was one of the first ways I became interested in computers.

I competed in games such as Call of Duty and enjoyed the challenge of improving my skills.

I also enjoyed modifying games and creating my own changes, which helped develop the same curiosity that later moved into programming.

Music & Learning

I enjoy expanding my knowledge and learning new skills. That mindset has led me into many different interests.

Learning guitar was another example of this. I enjoy music and wanted to challenge myself by learning an instrument.

Helping Others

While I enjoy technology, I believe the people side is just as important.

I enjoy helping people, explaining things clearly, and making difficult situations easier.

I try to bring patience, humour, and a positive attitude when working with others.
